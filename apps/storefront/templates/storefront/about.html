What we're building

FaizanMart is a marketplace platform where independent sellers list and manage their own stores, and shoppers browse, compare and check out across every seller in a single cart. Sellers get their own dashboard to manage products, orders and store settings; buyers get order tracking, wishlists, reviews, returns and a shared cart — regardless of how many different stores an order touches.
